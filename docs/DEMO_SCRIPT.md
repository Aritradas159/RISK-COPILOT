# 2-minute demo script

1. Start on the landing page: "We do not predict the market; we make risk visible."
2. Create/login to an account.
3. Open Simulator and choose SIP.
4. Enter a simple investment and duration.
5. Run 10,000 scenarios.
6. Show probability of loss, median outcome, percentile range and drawdown.
7. Point out the yearly fan chart (P10-median-P90 band growing over time) next to the fixed-deposit comparison line — this shows the *range* of outcomes over time, not just one final number.
8. Switch to the "Bad timing mode" tab and replay a real crash year (e.g. 2008) — no randomness, the actual historical sequence — to show what really happens to an unlucky entry point.
9. Switch to F&O and trigger the friction gate.
10. Open Learn and switch English → Tamil.
11. Return to History and show the saved simulation.
12. End with the disclaimer and the B2B pre-trade risk-check direction from the pitch.
